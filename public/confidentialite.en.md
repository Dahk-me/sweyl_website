# Privacy policy

_Effective date: {{privacyEffectiveDate}}_

_This translation is provided for convenience. Only the French version is legally binding._

Sweyl is a statistics tracking application for amateur basketball. It is used by clubs, their officials, their coaches and their players, **many of whom are minors**. This policy explains what data we process, why, with whom, for how long, and how to exercise your rights.

It applies to the www.sweyl.com website and to the Sweyl application, in its web version as well as in its versions installed from the App Store and Google Play.

## 2. The data we process

The categories below describe how the application actually works. Not all of them apply to every user: a viewer has no statistics, a club administrator has no body measurements.

### 2.1 Account and identity

- First name, last name, sex, and when provided: email address, date of birth, application language.
- Password: it is entrusted to the authentication provider. The publisher does not store it and cannot read it, and neither can the club.
- The profile or profiles held by the account (player, coach, club administrator, viewer), and the clubs and teams they belong to, with their arrival and departure dates. After a departure, the link is kept with its end date, for the sporting history.
- Licence photo: the club's coach or administrator can upload a photo of the player, specific to each club. It is deleted when the player leaves that club.

No phone number is requested from individuals.

### 2.2 Player's sporting profile

- Federation licence number (visible only to the team staff), age category, positions, preferred jersey number.
- Physical measurements, when entered: weight, height, height in shoes, wingspan, standing reach, date of the measurement.

### 2.3 Data created by the club

A club can register its members without them creating their own account, in particular by importing a file: first name, last name, date of birth, sex, email address, teams, jersey number and licence number. The email address is optional: a profile can therefore exist in the name of a person who has never signed in.

### 2.4 Sporting activity

- **Games**: line-up, number worn, playing time, statistics entered during or after the game (including the position of shots on the court and the matching moment in the video), substitutions.
- **Practices**: attendance (present, excused or unexcused absence), availability answer and any comment.
- **Shooting drills**: shots made and attempted, by date, on the team's drills and on the player's personal drills.
- **Measurement days (Combine)** organised by partner organisations: first name, last name, year of birth, declared club, bib number and results at the stations. The day's ranking shows first name, last name and bib number on a public screen at the event.

### 2.5 Staff assessments

The club's coaches and administrator can rate a player on a game (offence, defence, attitude), write or record aloud an assessment, and leave feedback on the game.

- The player reads their own ratings and assessments, and listens to their own voice assessments.
- The overall feedback on the game is visible to the team's roster.

### 2.6 Player follow-ups, including health data

A club can keep follow-ups on its players: injury, medical certificate, licence, school follow-up, equipment. A follow-up has a name, a comment, a **private comment**, its status, and attachments (PDF or image).

These follow-ups may contain **health data** and **school data**, often about minors. They are read by the player's coaches and the club administrator. The player sees their own, except the private comment, which they can obtain on request under their right of access (section 9). Their content is entered under the club's sole responsibility: the publisher provides the follow-up tool, it neither consults nor uses this information.

### 2.7 Videos, voice and uploaded content

- **Game videos**, when the team has the video option: uploaded to our storage servers, or kept on the user's device if they prefer. The clips and edits made from them record their author and the player concerned, and each viewing is recorded.
- **Voice recordings** by coaches (assessments, game feedback).
- **Play system videos**, logos of clubs and partner organisations.

Private files are only accessible through temporary links, issued to authorised members only.

### 2.8 Data about people outside Sweyl

- **Opposing players**: to prepare a game, a club's staff can keep a sheet on the players of an opposing team (first name, last name, number, year of birth, height, position, strong hand, notes, photo, videos). These sheets are visible only to the staff of the club that created them.
- **Directory of federation clubs**: we reuse the public contact details of clubs published by the French Basketball Federation, including the name, phone number and email address of their president and their correspondent.

These sheets are preparation notes entered by the club's staff, under its sole responsibility: the publisher provides the tool, it neither checks nor uses their content. Any person concerned can request the correction or deletion of the information about them (section 9).

### 2.9 Subscriptions and payments

Subscription requests, plan subscribed, start and end dates, decision taken on the request.

A subscription taken out in the application on iPhone, iPad or Android is paid to Apple or Google: we never receive your card details, only the confirmation of the purchase and the status of the subscription.

### 2.10 Notifications

The notifications received in the application, and, if you enable notifications on a device, the technical address your browser or phone provides to receive them (ten devices at most per account). You can turn them off in the settings, profile by profile.

### 2.11 Technical data

- **IP address**: read to limit abuse for the duration of the request, then forgotten. It is not recorded.
- **Error reports**: when an error occurs, a technical report is sent to our error tracking service. It describes the error and the request involved, and may contain information about the user concerned. It is used only to fix the application.
- **Data kept on your device**, needed for the application to work: sign-in session, active profile, language and theme, notification choice, display preferences, local copies of play system videos and voice recordings already listened to, access to game videos opened from the device.

### 2.12 What we do not collect

No location, no contacts, no camera, no advertising identifier. The microphone is used only while recording a voice assessment, at the coach's request. No audience measurement, advertising or cross-app tracking tool is built into the application.

## 3. Why we process it

- **Providing the service** (accounts, clubs, teams, statistics, practices, videos, follow-ups, notifications): performance of the contract between the user, or their club, and the publisher.
- **Ensuring security** (authentication, abuse limiting, error tracking, back-office log): the publisher's legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users.
- **Managing subscriptions**: performance of the contract and accounting obligations.
- **Producing the season recap**: feature suspended, no recap is produced any more (section 6).
- **Sending account-related messages** (invitation, password reset, address change): performance of the contract.

No data is sold, rented or used for advertising purposes.

## 4. Minors

A large share of the players registered on Sweyl are under 18. Their data receives particular attention.

- **Registration by the club.** When a club registers a minor player, it undertakes to have the consent of their legal guardians to include them in the application.
- **Age of digital consent.** In France, a minor under 15 cannot consent alone to the processing of their data when that processing is based on consent: the consent of one of the holders of parental authority is then required (article 45 of the French Data Protection Act).
- **Obtaining parental consent.** Under 13, the player's profile is held by a legal guardian's account; from 13 to 14, the player can have their own account with the consent of a legal guardian, recorded and kept; from 15, they use their account on their own. The age comes from the date of birth entered by the club, or is declared at sign-up and cannot be changed by the player afterwards.
- **One parent for several children.** A parent can hold each of their children's profiles on their email address.
- **Rights exercised by parents.** The legal guardians of a minor player can exercise on their behalf all the rights described in section 9, at {{privacyContact}}.
- **No public profile.** A player's data is accessible only to authorised members of their clubs. The only public pages are a club's game schedule, with no personal data, and the ranking screen of a measurement day (section 2.4).

## 5. Who can see your data

- **Within your club**, depending on your role. A club's data is never accessible to another club. Coaches see the players of their teams, the club administrator sees the whole club. A player sees their team's full box score, including their teammates' statistics.
- **The publisher**, to run the service, handle subscription requests and answer support requests. Access to the back office is restricted to the publisher, and every action there is logged.
- **Our technical processors**, strictly within the limits of their task (section 7).
- **The authorities**, only on a legally grounded request.

## 6. Artificial intelligence

**Season recap production is suspended**: no data is sent to an artificial intelligence service any more. Recaps already produced remain viewable in the application.

While it was active, to write the season recap, the application sent to an artificial intelligence service: for a player, their first name (never their last name or date of birth), their club, their team, the season, their statistics and up to ten assessments written by their coaches; for a coach, their first name, their club, their team and the season review. The text produced is kept in the application.

Provider: Anthropic PBC, United States.

No decision producing legal effects or significantly affecting a person is taken by automated means.

## 7. Processors and transfers outside the European Union

Each provider accesses only the data needed for its task and is bound by a contract compliant with article 28 of the GDPR.

- **Supabase**: database, authentication, sending account-related emails, real time, in Paris.
- **Mailjet** (France): delivery of account-related emails, and of the website's demo request form.
- **Fly.io**: application server, in Paris.
- **Vercel**: hosting of the website and the web application, served from a global network; the application's requests are forwarded to the Paris server.
- **Cloudflare**: file storage (videos, voice, photos, attachments), in spaces restricted to the European Union.
- **Sentry** (United States): technical error tracking.
- **GitHub** (United States): database backup copies.
- **Anthropic** (United States): season recap, see section 6.
- **Apple and Google**: payment of subscriptions taken out in the installed application, and delivery of notifications to devices. Microsoft and Mozilla also deliver notifications to their browsers.
- **YouTube and Vimeo**: playback of videos whose link a coach pastes. YouTube is embedded in cookie-free mode.

**Transfers outside the European Union.** Some providers are established in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, in particular the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, included in these providers' terms.

## 8. Retention periods

- Account and profile: kept as long as the account exists, erased within 30 days of the deletion request, except for the first name, last name and sporting history kept by the club (section 9). An account inactive for 3 years is deleted, after a warning email.
- Statistics and sporting history after leaving a club: kept with the box score, which is part of the club's history.
- Assessments, voice recordings and follow-ups: assessments and voice recordings are kept with the game; follow-ups are kept with the club's history, and their attachments concerning the player, which may contain health data, are deleted when their account is deleted.
- Game videos: kept as long as the team's video option is active, then deleted 90 days after it ends.
- Opponent scouting sheets: their videos are deleted three months after the opposing team is archived or the Pro option ends.
- Notifications: deleted three days after they expire.
- Database backup copies: 90 days.
- Subscription requests and accounting records: 10 years, the legal retention period for accounting records.
- Back-office log: 1 year.

Beyond that, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

## 9. Your rights

You have a right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection regarding your data, as well as the right to withdraw a consent given at any time and to set instructions regarding what happens to your data after your death.

**To exercise them**, write to {{privacyContact}}. You will receive an answer within one month, which may be extended by two months for a complex request. Proof of identity may be requested in case of reasonable doubt.

**Account deletion.** The process is in the application's settings. Deletion erases the account, the email address, the photo, the date of birth, the measurements, the follow-up attachments and everything the user created themselves (personal drills, clips, edits). The first name, last name and sporting history kept by the club (statistics, attendance, coaches' assessments and voice recordings) remain in the club, attached to a player without an account, whom the club administrator can invite again. A club administrator first hands the administration over to a coach of the club, who is informed of it; if the club has no coach, deleting the account closes the club. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription taken out with Apple or Google: it is cancelled in the settings of your Apple or Google account.

**Complaint.** If you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the French data protection authority (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés, CNIL), at www.cnil.fr.

## 10. Security

Exchanges between the application and our servers are encrypted. Data is encrypted at rest at our hosting providers. Access is restricted by role and by club, and checked by the server on every request. The database is backed up before each update and every week.

## 11. Cookies and local storage

The www.sweyl.com website sets no advertising or tracking cookie. It sets only two preference cookies, the chosen language and theme, and measures its audience without cookies (see the [legal notice](/mentions-legales)).

The application uses only the storage items strictly needed for it to work (section 2.11), which do not require prior consent.
