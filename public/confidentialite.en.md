# Privacy policy

_Effective date: {{privacyEffectiveDate}}_

_This translation is provided for convenience. Only the French version is legally binding._

Sweyl is a sports tracking application for amateur basketball, used by clubs, their officials, their coaches and their players, **many of whom are minors**. This policy explains what data we process, why, for how long, and how to exercise your rights.

It applies to the www.sweyl.com website and to the Sweyl application, on the web, the App Store and Google Play.

## 1. Data controller

Data is processed by **{{publisherName}}**, {{publisherLegalForm}}, {{publisherAddress}}, registered under number {{publisherRegistration}}. Contact: {{privacyContact}}.

**The club's role.** The club decides to register its teams, coaches and players, and what information it enters about them. It is responsible for it; the publisher provides the tool and processes this information on the club's behalf.

## 2. The data we process

- **Account and identity**: last name, first name, email address, date of birth, photo, and the account's clubs, teams and roles. The password is entrusted to our authentication provider: neither the publisher nor the club can read it. A club can register a member who has not yet created their account.
- **Sporting activity**: games, statistics, practices, attendance, physical measurements, and the coaches' written or voice assessments.
- **Player follow-ups**: a club can keep follow-ups on its players, which may contain **health** or school data. They are read by the player's staff; the player sees their own. Their content is entered under the club's sole responsibility.
- **Uploaded content**: videos, voice recordings, photos and documents, accessible only to authorised members.
- **People outside Sweyl**: a club's staff can take notes on opposing players, visible to that staff only and under its responsibility. We also reuse the public contact details of clubs published by the French Basketball Federation.
- **Subscriptions**: the plan subscribed and its dates. An in-app purchase is paid to Apple or Google: we never receive your card details.
- **Technical data**: error reports used to fix the application, and items kept on your device for it to work (session, preferences).

We collect no location, no contacts and no advertising identifier. The microphone is used only to record a voice comment. No advertising or tracking tool is built into the application.

## 3. Why we process it

- **Providing the service** and managing subscriptions: performance of the contract between the user, or their club, and the publisher.
- **Ensuring security** and fixing errors: the publisher's legitimate interest.
- **Meeting our legal obligations**, in particular accounting ones.

No data is sold, rented or used for advertising purposes.

## 4. Minors

- When a club registers a minor player, it undertakes to have the consent of their legal guardians.
- Under 15, a legal guardian's consent is required for the player to use their own account. A parent can also hold their children's profiles on their own address.
- Legal guardians can exercise the rights in section 7 on the minor's behalf.
- A player's data is visible only to authorised members of their clubs. There is no public profile.

## 5. Who can see your data

- **Your club**, depending on your role. A club's data is never accessible to another club.
- **The publisher**, to run the service and answer support requests.
- **Our technical providers** (hosting, storage, email delivery, notifications, error tracking, payment), strictly within the limits of their task and under contract.
- **The authorities**, only on a legally grounded request.

Some providers are located outside the European Union. These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided by the GDPR, in particular the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 6. Retention periods

- The account is kept as long as it exists, and erased within 30 days of a deletion request. An account inactive for 3 years is deleted, after a warning email.
- The sporting history (games, statistics, attendance, assessments) remains in the club's history.
- Videos are deleted after the option hosting them ends.
- Accounting records are kept for 10 years, the legal period.

## 7. Your rights

You have a right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection regarding your data. To exercise them, write to {{privacyContact}}: you will receive an answer within one month.

**Account deletion.** It is done from the application's settings. It erases the account and what the user created themselves; the first name, last name and sporting history kept by the club remain in the club. It does not cancel a subscription taken out with Apple or Google, which is cancelled in that account's settings.

**Complaint.** You can contact the French data protection authority (CNIL), at www.cnil.fr.

## 8. Security

Exchanges are encrypted, data is encrypted at rest, and access is checked by the server, by role and by club, on every request.

## 9. Cookies

The www.sweyl.com website sets no advertising or tracking cookie, only two preference cookies (language and theme) (see the [legal notice](/mentions-legales)). The application uses only the storage strictly needed for it to work.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change along with the service. In the event of a significant change, users are informed in the application or by email.
